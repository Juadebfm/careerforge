Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about CareerForge plans and billing.

Can I switch or upgrade at any time?

Yes. You can start on the Free plan today and choose to upgrade whenever you require unlimited job tracking or the AI Career Coach. Switching is immediate and hassle-free.

What happens when I reach the 5-job limit on Free?

Your 5 active jobs remain fully editable, readable, and tracked. To add additional opportunities, you can either archive older applications or upgrade to CareerForge Pro for unlimited slots.

How does the AI Career Coach help with my applications?

The coach reads your current learning milestones and job descriptions to provide tailored talking points, role competency breakdowns, and customized technical interview practice questions.

Do I need prior coding experience to use CareerForge?

CareerForge is tailored for learners transitioning into software engineering or leveling up to their next junior-to-mid developer role. The Full-Stack roadmap starts with modern basics and progresses systematically.

Is there a student discount available?

We are committed to keeping career acceleration accessible to aspiring engineers. We plan to support verified students and recent bootcamp graduates once Pro pricing is officially finalized.
